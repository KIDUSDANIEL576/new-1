Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Trace is a drawing app for two people. This policy says exactly what we store, why, and what we never touch. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What we collect, and why

  • Email address and a password — your account. The password is stored only as a cryptographic hash.
  • A display name — what your partner sees.
  • Your drawings — every stroke you draw (its shape, colour, brush, and time) is stored so it can appear on your partner's device and be replayed later. That is the product.
  • Photos you choose to upload as canvas backgrounds — stored privately, visible only to you and your partner.
  • A push notification token for your device, so your partner's drawings and rings can reach you.
  • Buzz timestamps — when a ring was sent and to whom, kept only to rate-limit ringing. No content, because a buzz has none.
  • Purchase records — if you buy Trace Forever, the store and date of purchase, so both of you stay unlocked.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no data sales. None.
  • The live camera glimpse is never stored. When you turn the camera on, frames travel directly to your partner and are discarded on arrival. They are never written to a server, a database, or a disk. Audio is never captured at all.
  • Your partner cannot export your drawings. Your ink is yours; each of you can export only your own.
  • We cannot read your invisible-ink secrets on your home screen. Secret-brush strokes are excluded from widget images by design.

Where your data lives

All data is stored with Supabase (Postgres database and file storage), protected by row-level security so each couple's data is reachable only by its two members. Push notifications are delivered via Expo's push service, and purchases are processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat — payment card details never touch us.

Your rights

  • Export — download everything you've made (your account details, your strokes as data and as images) from the account panel, any time.
  • Delete — delete your account from inside the app. This is real deletion: your login, drawings, streak, and tokens are erased. Your partner keeps their own drawings.
  • Leave — leave a shared canvas without deleting your account; your drawings go with you.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, because the shared canvas history is the product. Delete your account and it is removed immediately, not archived.

Children

Trace is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and material changes will be announced inside the app before they take effect.
