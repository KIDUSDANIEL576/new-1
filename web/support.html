How do we pair up?

One of you signs in and taps Start our canvas — you'll get a 6-character code. The other signs in with their own email, taps I have a code, and enters it. That's it, forever.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot your password? — a reset link goes to your email. Open it, choose a new password, and you're back in.

The Ring button says "give them a moment"

That's on purpose. Ringing someone's phone is loud, so it's limited to one ring every 2 minutes per person. The countdown shows when you can ring again.

Why won't my phone ring on silent?

Trace respects silent mode and Do Not Disturb — the platforms reserve breaking through silence for emergency and medical apps, and we think that's right. On a phone that isn't silenced, a buzz rings out loud.

Some brushes have a lock on them

Marker and Chalk are free forever. Glow, Neon, and the Secret (invisible ink) brush are part of Trace Forever — one purchase that unlocks both of you at once, permanently.

What's the Secret brush?

It writes something your partner can see is there — a soft blur — but can't read until they press and hold hold to read. It reveals for six seconds, then hides itself again. Secrets never appear on home-screen widgets.

How do I add the widget?

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → find Trace → add it. It shows your shared canvas and updates as you two draw.

How do I get my data out, or delete my account?

Long-press the trace wordmark (top-left in the app) to open your account. Download my data gives you everything you've made — your drawings as images and data. Delete my account is at the bottom, in red, and it genuinely deletes: your login and your drawings are erased, your partner keeps theirs. See the privacy policy for the full details.
